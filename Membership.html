Shadowing Opportunities

Virtual Shadowing

Join us for a Zoom-based meeting where incoming freshmen can learn about Engineering House, meet current members, and ask questions from the comfort of your own home.

In-Person Shadowing

Spend a day with a current member — attend classes together, get a tour of the floor, and experience what life at Engineering House is really like.

Contact: ehouserecruitment@gmail.com

Types of Membership

On-Floor Members

On-floor members live on the Engineering House floor in DSP. You'll enjoy an open-door culture with access to the VAX workshop, study lounges, pool lounge, and a tight-knit community of fellow engineers right next door.

Off-Floor Members

Off-floor members have access to all the same events, resources, and community — just without living on the floor. The recruitment chair will work with you to find ways to stay connected, and if a spot opens up, you may have the opportunity to move on-floor.

Express interest: Off-Floor Signup Form

How Do I Join?

To join Engineering House, apply through your RIT housing contract during room selection. A selection committee reviews all applications. Make sure to list Engineering House as your preference in the RIT Housing application!
