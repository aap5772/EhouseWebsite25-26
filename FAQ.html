Engineering House offers a supportive community of like-minded students, access to workshop spaces, study lounges, social events throughout the year, Imagine RIT project opportunities, and lifelong friendships with fellow engineers.
We expect members to participate in house activities and contribute to the community. This includes attending house meetings, participating in events, and maintaining a positive presence on the floor.
Engineering House is a Special Interest House (SIH) that occupies one floor in the DSP residence hall (formerly NRH). It's a living-learning community where students who are passionate about engineering and technology live together.
Off-floor members participate in all the same events and have access to the same resources. The recruitment chair helps off-floor members stay connected with the community, and there may be opportunities to move on-floor if spots become available.
No worries! You can still join Engineering House. Talk to the recruitment chair about your specific situation and we'll work with you to find the best arrangement.
Off-floor membership is $40/semester and on-floor membership is $90/semester. These dues go toward house events, supplies, and improvements.
