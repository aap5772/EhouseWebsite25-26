WHO WE ARE

We Are Engineering House

Engineering House is a Special Interest House at RIT focused on collaboration, innovation, and community. Founded in 1971 and located in DSP (formerly NRH), we bring together students who are passionate about engineering, technology, and making things.

We're a tight-knit community that supports each other academically and personally. Whether you need help studying for an exam, want feedback on a project, or just need someone to grab dinner with — there's always someone around.

From beach days and ski trips to hackathons and formal dinners, Engineering House offers a full spectrum of experiences that create lasting friendships and memories throughout your time at RIT.
